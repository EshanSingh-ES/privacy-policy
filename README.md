# Privacy Policy – FloatBrowse

Effective Date: November 2026

FloatBrowse is a lightweight floating web browser for macOS that allows you to browse the web seamlessly while working in other apps. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

## Your Browsing Stays Private

FloatBrowse does not collect your browsing history. The pages you visit, what you type, your bookmarks, and your tabs are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

## Information We Collect

To offer subscriptions, FloatBrowse uses Superwall, a paywall and subscription service. Superwall receives:

- A randomly generated device identifier that is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID
- Anonymous app events, such as when a subscription screen is shown, dismissed, or a purchase is completed
- Basic device information such as your operating system version and country

This information is never linked to your browsing activity, and Superwall never receives the pages you visit.

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

## Third-Party Services

FloatBrowse does not include advertising or cross-app tracking frameworks, and we do not sell your data.

The services we use are:

- **Superwall** — subscriptions and paywalls ([privacy policy](https://superwall.com/privacy))
- **Apple** — payment processing and subscription management

Websites you visit may collect their own data according to their respective privacy policies.

## Data Security

The data described above is transmitted over encrypted connections. Because FloatBrowse does not collect your browsing activity or personal identity, there is no browsing history or personal profile that could be exposed.

## Your Choices

You can stop all subscription-related data collection by not using the subscription features. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in System Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
